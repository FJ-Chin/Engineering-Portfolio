03 / Internships

Internship work that improves real workflows.

At CellWave, I built a computer-vision cartridge alignment system for cell sorting and proposed a PDMS mould workflow that reduced inconsistent manual cutting and punching.

At KOKONI, I worked on Mixin, an automatic CMYK paint mixer for hobbyists, and an AI image-to-3D workflow that helps users turn sketches into editable Blender files.

These experiences shaped how I think about product development: solve the workflow, reduce friction, and make the result easy to repeat.
